# Flowcast Design System: Warm Reassuring Companion

> **Stitch Project**: Flowcast (`projects/2782599774129089756`)  
> **Origin**: Google Stitch  
> **Primary Font Family**: Plus Jakarta Sans  
> **Color Mode**: Light  

---

## 1. Brand & Style Philosophy

This design system is crafted for a protective, mindful travel companion. The design communicates emotional grounding, safety, and effortless clarity during transit, orientation, and unforeseen travel friction. The target audience comprises solo voyagers, cautious planners, and lifestyle travelers who seek confidence and peace of mind rather than high-octane stimulation or cold administrative metrics.

The aesthetic philosophy fuses **Warm Tactile Minimalism** with **Organic Modernism**:
- **Generous Breathing Room:** Expansive whitespace paired with a soft, parchment-inspired tonal base evokes physical stationery and calm daylight.
- **Supportive & Gentle:** High tactile reassurance, free from alarmist visuals or stark institutional rigidity. Critical states reassure rather than induce panic.
- **Refined Balance:** Crisp functional cards layered over organic canvas hues, paired with geometric yet human typography that balances legibility with warmth.

## 3. Typography Hierarchy

Flowcast leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all textual hierarchies, chosen for its friendly apertures, humane geometric proportion, and clear readability at both glance and read distances.

### 3.1 Type Scale Specification

| Token | Size | Line Height | Weight | Letter Spacing | Primary Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `display` | 48px | 56px | 700 (Bold) | -0.03em | Hero headlines, landing greetings, large metrics |
| `display-mobile` | 34px | 42px | 700 (Bold) | -0.02em | Hero headers on mobile devices |
| `headline-lg` | 32px | 40px | 700 (Bold) | -0.02em | Main page headers, onboarding screen titles |
| `headline-lg-mobile` | 26px | 34px | 700 (Bold) | -0.015em | Screen titles on mobile viewports |
| `headline-md` | 24px | 32px | 600 (Semi-bold) | -0.015em | Major section headers, card modal titles |
| `headline-sm` | 20px | 28px | 600 (Semi-bold) | -0.01em | Sub-section headings, trip milestone titles |
| `title-lg` | 18px | 26px | 600 (Semi-bold) | -0.005em | Card titles, group labels, dialog headers |
| `body-lg` | 18px | 28px | 400 (Regular) | 0 | Lead paragraphs, advisory intros |
| `body-md` | 15px | 24px | 400 (Regular) | 0 | Standard interface text, forms, instructions |
| `body-sm` | 13px | 20px | 400 (Regular) | +0.005em | Secondary annotations, footnotes, ancillary details |
| `label-md` | 14px | 20px | 600 (Semi-bold) | +0.01em | Button labels, tabs, input field labels |
| `label-sm` | 12px | 16px | 600 (Semi-bold) | +0.02em | Status pill chips, uppercase tags, telemetry |
| `caption` | 11px | 14px | 500 (Medium) | +0.03em | Micro-timestamps, legal disclaimers, badges |

### 3.2 Typographic Principles
- **Headlines & Titles:** Set with tightened tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.01em`) and confident weights (600 and 700) to project safety, stability, and structure without aggressive punchiness.
- **Body Text:** Renders in regular weight (400) with generous line heights (`1.55` to `1.6`) against the neutral charcoal tone (`#1F1B15` / `#4A453E`). This reduces eye strain when reading advisories, flight statuses, or medical resources in low-light travel environments.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Utilize medium-to-semibold weights with neutral-to-expanded tracking for immediate readability on pill badges, map indicators, and transit telemetry.

## 4. Layout, Spacing & Grid System

The layout follows a **Disciplined Fluid-Grid with Airy Compositional Safe-Zones**, organized around a strict 8px harmonic baseline.

### 4.1 Spacing Scale

| Spacing Token | Value (rem / px) | Typical Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `space-xxs` | `0.25rem` (4px) | Micro gaps between icons and adjacent text |
| `space-xs` | `0.5rem` (8px) | Tight inner component padding, chip padding |
| `space-sm` | `0.75rem` (12px) | Compact form control padding, list item gaps |
| `space-md` | `1.0rem` (16px) | Standard component padding, gutter base |
| `space-lg` | `1.5rem` (24px) | Card internal padding, block separation |
| `space-xl` | `2.0rem` (32px) | Section gaps, modal padding |
| `space-2xl` | `3.0rem` (48px) | Major page section offsets, content breaks |
| `space-3xl` | `4.5rem` (72px) | Hero section vertical breathing room |

### 4.2 Breakpoints & Grids

- **Mobile (`< 768px`)**:
  - Columns: 4
  - Outer Margin: `1.25rem` (20px)
  - Gutters: `1.0rem` (16px)
  - Navigation: Pinned bottom bar within easy thumb reach.
- **Tablet (`768px - 1024px`)**:
  - Columns: 8
  - Outer Margin: `2.0rem` (32px)
  - Gutters: `1.5rem` (24px)
  - Layout: Dual-pane inspection views (e.g., itinerary list beside interactive map advisory).
- **Desktop (`> 1024px`)**:
  - Columns: 12
  - Outer Margin: `3.5rem` (56px)
  - Gutters: `2.0rem` (32px)
  - Content Max-Width: `1200px` (centered with generous warm cream margins to prevent cognitive overload).

## 5. Elevation, Depth & Glassmorphism

Flowcast avoids harsh drop shadows and artificial 3D layering, opting for **Warm Ambient Diffusion and Low-Contrast Perimeter Definition**:

- **Ground (Level 0):** Soft cream canvas (`#FFF8F3` / `#FAF7F2`), non-elevated.
- **Resting Surface (Level 1 - Cards, Modules):**
  - Background: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`)
  - Border: 1px hairline border (`#E8E4DC` or `#E1BFBA`)
  - Shadow:
    ```css
    box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(74, 69, 62, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(74, 69, 62, 0.02);
    ```
- **Floating Surface (Level 2 - Hovered Cards, Modals, Dropdowns):**
  - Border: 1px hairline border (`#DDD7CD`)
  - Shadow:
    ```css
    box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(74, 69, 62, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(74, 69, 62, 0.03);
    ```
- **Luminescent Accent Layer (Primary Action Focus / Hover):**
  - Warm Coral ambient halo:
    ```css
    box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(255, 107, 87, 0.28);
    ```
- **Glassmorphism Modifiers:**
  - Pinned mobile headers and contextual bottom sheets apply `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` over an 85% alpha cream ground:
    ```css
    background: rgba(250, 247, 242, 0.85);
    backdrop-filter: blur(16px);
    ```

## 7. Component Guidelines

### 7.1 Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Warm Coral background (`#FF6B57`), crisp white text, fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) or `12px` rounded corner. Padding: `12px 24px`. Hover transitions upward with a `-1px` shift and a Coral glow (`box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(255, 107, 87, 0.28)`).
- **Secondary Button:** Pure white fill (`#FFFFFF`), 1px perimeter outline (`#DDD7CD`), neutral text (`#4A453E`). Hover state tint: `#F5F0E8`.
- **Destructive/Emergency Variant:** High-visibility coral wash with an alert icon; maintains rounded geometry to preserve composure.

### 7.2 Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height 48px, 12px border radius, subtle warm background (`#F5F0E8`), border 1px solid transparent. On focus: transitions to white surface (`#FFFFFF`), border becomes Coral (`#FF6B57`), with a gentle 3px focus ring (`rgba(255, 107, 87, 0.15)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 20px base with 6px border radius (checkbox) or circular (radio). Unchecked: `#E8E4DC` border. Checked: Calm Teal fill (`#0F9B8E`) with white internal glyph.
- **Soft Pill Toggles:** Height 28px, width 50px, rounded to `9999px`. Subtle track (`#E8E4DC`), active track (`#0F9B8E`), handle is a floating crisp white circle with ambient shadow.

### 7.3 Cards & Modules
- **Base Styling:** White fill (`#FFFFFF`), 16px border-radius, 1px border (`#E8E4DC`), ambient level-1 shadow. Internal card padding: 20px (mobile) to 28px (desktop).
- **Header Structure:** Header sections within cards include muted secondary meta tags and contextual pill badges.

### 7.4 Chips & Status Pill Badges
- Height 26px, rounded to `9999px`, padding: 4px 12px.
- **Safe / Active:** Teal surface (`rgba(15, 155, 142, 0.1)`), Calm Teal text (`#0F9B8E`).
- **Notice / Pending:** Amber surface (`rgba(244, 162, 97, 0.1)`), Amber text (`#D47A32`).
- **Alert / Action Required:** Coral surface (`rgba(255, 107, 87, 0.1)`), Coral text (`#FF6B57`).

### 7.5 Specialized Travel Companion: SafeStatus Banner
- A reassuring horizontal card anchored with a Calm Teal or Warm Coral pulse dot (8px circular dot with a radiating 2s CSS ping animation), indicating real-time journey check-in and regional status at a single glance.
