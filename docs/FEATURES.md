# Flowcast Features Breakdown

## 1. Attendee Experience Module
- **Live Itinerary & Timeline**: Adaptive schedule with crowd density warnings.
- **Interactive Map & Pins**: Live zone status indicators (Green, Yellow, Red).
- **Smart Suggestions & Alternatives**: Timely nudges recommending off-peak meal slots or alternative transit gates.
- **Notifications Hub**: Push alerts for schedule changes, venue alerts, and personalized re-routing options.
- **Trip Summary & Confirmation**: Seamless confirmation tracking for reserved transport and accommodation options.
- **Quick Onboarding Flow**: 2-step onboarding to capture mobility preferences, dietary needs, and session priorities.

## 2. Organizer Command Center Module
- **Real-time Command Center**: Live overview of total attendees, active zones, peak congestions, and active incident response.
- **Simulation Engine**: What-if scenario testing (e.g., heavy rain, gate 3 closure, transit delay) to assess ripple effects.
- **Dynamic Alert Dispatcher**: Broadcast urgent alerts to specific attendee segments or zones.
- **Accommodation Management**: Capacity tracking and alternative lodging allocation for multi-day events.
- **Transport Hub Telemetry**: Shuttle bus frequencies, gate queues, and route congestion metrics.
- **Venue & Zone Configurations**: Capacity limits, threshold alerts, and live sensor integration.
