# Problem Statement: PS-8

## Title: Intelligent Event Crowd & Logistical Flow Optimization

### Context
Major events (conferences, mega-expos, sports tournaments, festivals) suffer from chronic logistical friction:
1. **Bottlenecks & Sudden Overcrowding**: Attendees cluster around popular booths, keynote halls, or transit hubs.
2. **Asymmetric Resource Utilization**: Food stalls, alternative sessions, or secondary transit gates remain underutilized while primary zones hit code violations.
3. **Attendee Stress & Time Loss**: Lack of real-time visibility into transit congestion, line wait times, and dynamic itinerary alternatives.
4. **Organizer Blindspots**: Post-incident reaction rather than pre-emptive scenario simulation and proactive attendee guidance.

### Flowcast Solution
Flowcast introduces a two-sided coordination engine:
- **For Organizers**: A real-time digital twin command center capable of live occupancy telemetry, threshold alerts, and what-if simulation to re-route logistics before gridlock forms.
- **For Attendees**: Personalized intelligent itineraries that adapt based on live crowd metrics, offering incentives and alternative timing to distribute footfall harmoniously.
